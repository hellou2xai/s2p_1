# Course 20: ESG and Sustainable Procurement

## The Scope 3 deadline

It is Monday morning. Your CPO forwards an email from the Chief Sustainability Officer: "As you know, we committed to Science Based Targets last quarter. We need a Scope 3 baseline by June 30. That means we need ESG assessments from our top 60 suppliers. We have 22 questionnaires back. Some are incomplete. The board deadline is eight weeks from today."

You open the shared drive. Twenty-two questionnaire responses in various formats. Some are PDFs, some are Word documents, some are emailed bullet points. Five are missing entire sections. Two suppliers scored themselves at 95/100 on everything, which is not credible. Three did not answer the carbon emissions section at all.

Manually processing each response takes about 40 minutes: read the document, extract scores, check completeness, estimate missing data, compute weighted totals, write the assessment summary. At 40 minutes each, the 22 responses alone will take 15 hours. And you still need to score the other 38 suppliers who have not responded.

This course builds the processing pipeline. You design an ESG framework, process supplier questionnaires (complete and incomplete), estimate Scope 3 emissions using the spend-based method, score suppliers against weighted criteria, and produce a portfolio dashboard with individual action plans.

## The practice scenario

You are the Sustainable Procurement Lead at a US-based manufacturer. Your organization committed to Science Based Targets. Your CPO has a Scope 3 reduction objective.

Today's date is **2026-04-25**. You have:

- 15 supplier assessments (10 complete, 5 partial with missing sections).
- Spend data for all 15 suppliers with emission factors by category.
- An ESG framework with 6 dimensions and weights.
- Industry benchmarks for context.
- Two suppliers with scores below 50 (Pacific Aluminum, Eagle Transport).

## The six lessons

| # | Title | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ESG framework design: dimensions, weights, thresholds | 40 min |
| 2 | Assessment processing: extracting data from inconsistent responses | 50 min |
| 3 | Scope 3 carbon estimation: spend-based method | 50 min |
| 4 | ESG scoring and red flag detection | 45 min |
| 5 | Supplier action plans: three highest-impact actions per supplier | 40 min |
| 6 | Portfolio dashboard and board reporting | 35 min |

Total: about 4.5 hours.

## You are done with the course when

1. You have processed all 15 assessments, including estimations for the 5 incomplete ones.
2. Each supplier has a weighted ESG score and a red flag if below the 40-point minimum threshold.
3. Your Scope 3 estimate uses spend-based emission factors with documented methodology.
4. Each supplier has an action plan with three prioritized improvements and a 12-month timeline.
5. Your portfolio dashboard shows score distribution, top and bottom performers, and SBTi progress narrative.
