# CFO Memo Solution

## Apex Procurement: Savings Program Update
**Date**: 2026-04-25
**Period**: Q1-Q3 2026 (January through September)
**Prepared by**: Savings Program Manager

### Headline

The savings program has realized **$8.2M** through Q3, representing **68.3%** of the $12M annual target. On a base-case projection, we expect to close the year at **$10.8M** (90.0% of target). Two initiatives require action to close the remaining gap.

### Variance analysis

**SAV-002 (Logistics RFP)**: Carrier negotiations extended 6 weeks due to capacity constraints in the Southeast lane. New rates effective August 1 instead of June 1. Two months of savings lost. Q4 run rate will partially recover.

**SAV-006 (Demand Reduction)**: Travel policy enforcement inconsistent across three business units. Actual reduction is 40% of target. Recommend mandatory pre-approval for travel above $2,000.

**SAV-007 (Spec Standardization)**: Engineering approval process added 8 weeks to the timeline. Three of seven specifications approved. Remaining four in review. Full-year savings will reach approximately 55% of target.

### Three scenarios

| Scenario | Full-year estimate | % of target | Key assumption |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base case | $10,800,000 | 90.0% | Current run rates continue. No recovery on SAV-006/SAV-007. |
| Upside | $11,400,000 | 95.0% | SAV-002 recovers 50% of lost months. Travel policy enforced in Q4. |
| Risk-adjusted | $9,900,000 | 82.5% | SAV-002 slips further. No recovery on SAV-006/SAV-007. |

### Three actions

1. **Enforce travel pre-approval** for trips above $2,000 across all business units. Estimated Q4 recovery: $160,000. Owner: CFO (policy) and Procurement (monitoring).
2. **Escalate engineering spec reviews** for SAV-007. Request dedicated engineering resource for remaining four specifications. Estimated recovery: $280,000 if approved by May 15.
3. **Accelerate SAV-002 carrier transition** by running parallel negotiations with backup carriers. Reduce dependency on the Southeast lane incumbent.
