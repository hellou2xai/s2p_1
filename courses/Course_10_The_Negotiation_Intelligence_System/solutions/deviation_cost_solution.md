# Deviation Cost Analysis: Redline Logistics LLC Proposal (Reference Solution)

**Contract**: CTR-2024-LG-001
**Current annual value**: $9,200,000
**Date**: 2026-04-25

## Deviation 1: Price increase (+12%)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Current annual cost | $9,200,000 |
| Proposed annual cost | $10,304,000 |
| Annual impact | +$1,104,000 |
| Over 12-month renewal | +$1,104,000 |
| Market-aligned increase (3.3%) | +$303,600 |
| Excess above market | +$800,400 |

**Assessment**: The $1,104,000 increase is $800,400 above what market benchmarks support. A 3.3% increase ($303,600) aligns with the national average rate movement. The supplier's cost justification contains two inflated claims: fuel surcharges (claimed 14.6%, actual 9.7%) and labor costs (claimed 8.2%, national index 6.8%).

## Deviation 2: Force majeure (narrowed)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Clause removed | "Supply chain disruptions beyond the reasonable control of the affected party" |
| Estimated risk exposure | $460,000 to $920,000 per event |
| Probability of occurrence (12 months) | 15% to 25% based on 2024-2025 incident frequency |
| Expected annual cost | $69,000 to $230,000 |

**Assessment**: Removing "supply chain disruptions" from force majeure shifts the full cost of any disruption event to TransGlobal. In the past 24 months, Redline experienced two events that could qualify (Atlanta hub system outage in April 2025, Dallas AC failure in August 2025). Each resulted in 2 days of service degradation. The estimated cost per event is 5% to 10% of monthly contract value ($460,000 to $920,000 annualized).

## Deviation 3: Auto-renewal notice (shortened)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Current notice period | 180 days |
| Proposed notice period | 90 days |
| Strategic cost | Reduced time to source alternatives |
| Financial cost (if trapped in unfavorable renewal) | $0 to $1,104,000 |

**Assessment**: A 90-day notice period gives TransGlobal less time to run a competitive process if renewal terms are unfavorable. A full sourcing cycle for logistics services of this scale takes 14 to 18 weeks (98 to 126 days). With a 90-day notice window, TransGlobal cannot complete a competitive process before the auto-renewal triggers. This effectively locks TransGlobal into the renewal terms unless it begins sourcing before the contract terms are finalized.

The combined proposed changes represent a 12.8% to 26.5% increase in total cost of the relationship, accounting for both direct pricing and risk-adjusted costs.
