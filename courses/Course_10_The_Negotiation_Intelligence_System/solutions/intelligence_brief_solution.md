# Intelligence Brief: Redline Logistics LLC Contract Renewal (Reference Solution)

**Prepared for**: VP of Procurement, TransGlobal Industries
**Date**: 2026-04-25
**Contract**: CTR-2024-LG-001
**Decision deadline**: 2026-05-07

## Executive summary

Redline Logistics LLC has proposed a 12% price increase ($1,104,000/year) on the $9.2M logistics contract, along with a narrowed force majeure clause and a shortened auto-renewal notice period. Market benchmarks do not support a 12% increase. The market average rate increase across all three service lines is 3.1% to 3.4% year-over-year. Supplier performance has declined: on-time delivery dropped from 96.8% in June 2024 to 93.1% in April 2026, breaching the 95.0% SLA target for six consecutive months.

## Market intelligence

Three findings from benchmark data:

1. **National logistics rates increased 3.1% to 3.4% year-over-year.** Redline's proposed 12% increase is 3.5x the market average. A market-aligned increase would be $287,000 to $313,000 per year, not $1,104,000.
2. **Redline's cost claims are partially inflated.** Redline claims 8.2% labor cost growth. The national index shows 6.8%. Redline claims 14.6% fuel surcharge growth. The actual index shows 9.7%.
3. **Two alternative providers quoted within 4% of current rates.** Summit Freight Solutions quoted $9,540,000/year (+3.7%). Ironclad Distribution quoted $9,180,000/year (-0.2%). Both are viable alternatives.

## Supplier performance assessment

| KPI | Contract target | Jun 2024 (start) | Apr 2026 (current) | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| On-time delivery | 95.0% | 96.8% | 93.1% | Declining. Below SLA since Oct 2025. |
| Damage rate | 0.5% | 0.28% | 0.47% | Rising. Approaching SLA threshold. |
| Invoice accuracy | 98.0% | 99.1% | 97.8% | Declining. Below SLA since Nov 2025. |
| Response time | 4.0 hours | 1.9 hours | 3.1 hours | Rising. Still within SLA. |

Six consecutive months of on-time delivery below the 95.0% SLA target. This triggers service credits and strengthens TransGlobal's negotiating position.

## Negotiation history patterns

From eight prior logistics negotiations:

- Average supplier opening position: 9.3% increase.
- Average final outcome: 3.8% increase.
- Average concession from opening: 5.5 percentage points.
- Redline's opening position (12%) is the second highest in the data set.
- The last Redline negotiation (NEG-005, Jan 2025) started at 7.0% and settled at 3.0%.
- One negotiation (NEG-007) resulted in a full provider switch when the supplier opened at 15%.

## Key negotiation advantages

1. **SLA breach record.** Six months of on-time delivery below target. Service credits owed.
2. **Inflated cost justification.** Fuel surcharge claim overstated by 4.9 percentage points.
3. **Viable alternatives.** Two providers quoted within 4% of current rates. Switching is credible.
