# Counter-Proposal: Redline Logistics LLC (Reference Solution)

**Date**: 2026-04-28
**From**: TransGlobal Industries, Procurement
**To**: Marcus Chen, VP of Sales, Redline Logistics LLC
**Re**: Counter-proposal for CTR-2024-LG-001 renewal

## Opening position

TransGlobal Industries values its partnership with Redline Logistics LLC and intends to renew contract CTR-2024-LG-001. We have reviewed the proposed renewal terms dated 2026-04-23 and offer the following counter-proposal.

## Counter-proposal terms

### 1. Pricing: 3.5% increase ($322,000/year)

**Redline proposed**: 12.0% increase ($1,104,000/year)
**TransGlobal counters**: 3.5% increase ($322,000/year)

Revised rate table:

| Service | Current rate | Counter rate | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warehousing | $14.20/pallet/month | $14.70/pallet/month | +3.5% |
| Last-mile delivery | $38.50/delivery | $39.85/delivery | +3.5% |
| Cross-dock | $6.80/pallet | $7.04/pallet | +3.5% |
| Management fee | $309,333/month | $320,160/month | +3.5% |

**Rationale**: Market benchmarks show national logistics rates increased 3.1% to 3.4% year-over-year across all three service categories. Our counter of 3.5% exceeds the market average and reflects TransGlobal's recognition of cost pressures. We note that the fuel surcharge increase claimed (14.6%) exceeds the actual diesel price index increase (9.7%) over the contract period.

### 2. Force majeure: retain current clause

**Redline proposed**: Remove "supply chain disruptions" from force majeure.
**TransGlobal counters**: Retain the current force majeure clause, including "supply chain disruptions beyond the reasonable control of the affected party."

**Rationale**: Supply chain disruptions remain a relevant force majeure event. The current clause has been in place since contract inception with no abuse. Removing it would shift an estimated $69,000 to $230,000 in annual expected risk to TransGlobal without a corresponding price offset.

### 3. Auto-renewal notice: 150 days

**Redline proposed**: 90 days.
**TransGlobal counters**: 150 days.

**Rationale**: TransGlobal requires sufficient lead time to evaluate renewal terms and, if necessary, conduct a competitive sourcing process. A 150-day notice period balances operational planning for both parties while preserving TransGlobal's ability to test the market.

### 4. New term: SLA improvement

**TransGlobal proposes**: Increase the on-time delivery SLA target from 95.0% to 95.5%.

**Rationale**: On-time delivery performance has declined from 96.8% (June 2024) to 93.1% (April 2026), falling below the 95.0% target for six consecutive months. A higher target reflects the performance standard TransGlobal requires and that Redline achieved during the first 10 months of the contract.

## Service credit reconciliation

TransGlobal requests reconciliation of service credits owed for the period October 2025 through April 2026 (six months of on-time delivery below the 95.0% SLA target). Estimated credits: $142,000 based on the 1.5% per-point penalty structure in Section 3 of the current MSA.

## Response

TransGlobal requests a written response by **2026-05-02** to allow time for further discussion before the 2026-05-07 deadline.
