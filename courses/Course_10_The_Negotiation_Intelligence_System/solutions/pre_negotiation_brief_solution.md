# Pre-Negotiation Brief: Redline Logistics LLC (Reference Solution)

**Prepared for**: VP of Procurement, TransGlobal Industries
**Supplier**: Redline Logistics LLC, Indianapolis, IN
**Contract**: CTR-2024-LG-001, $9,200,000/year
**Decision deadline**: 2026-05-07

## Situation

Redline Logistics LLC proposed a 12% price increase ($1,104,000/year), narrowed force majeure, and shortened auto-renewal notice on the $9.2M logistics contract. The combined annual exposure is $1.17M to $2.44M. Market data does not support the increase. Supplier performance has declined below SLA targets for six consecutive months.

## Recommendations

1. **Counter at 3.5% ($322,000/year).** This aligns with the national rate increase of 3.1% to 3.4%, plus a small premium for the incumbent relationship. Ceiling: 5.0% ($460,000/year). Walk-away: above 6.0%.
2. **Reject the force majeure change.** Retain the current clause including "supply chain disruptions." If Redline insists, require a $200,000 annual risk credit to offset the transferred exposure.
3. **Counter the notice period at 150 days.** This gives TransGlobal enough time to run a competitive process (14 to 18 weeks) if needed. Do not accept anything below 120 days.

## Negotiation positions

| Issue | Our opening | Our target | Walk-away |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price increase | 2.5% ($230,000) | 3.5% ($322,000) | 6.0% ($552,000) |
| Force majeure | No change | No change | Accept with $200,000 credit |
| Notice period | 180 days (no change) | 150 days | 120 days |
| SLA improvement | Add 96.0% on-time target | 95.5% target | 95.0% (current) |

## Negotiation advantages to raise

1. **Six months of SLA breaches.** On-time delivery below 95.0% since October 2025. Service credits owed. This weakens Redline's justification for a price increase.
2. **Inflated cost claims.** Redline claimed 14.6% fuel surcharge growth. Actual: 9.7%. Redline claimed 8.2% labor cost growth. National index: 6.8%. Present these figures early.
3. **Viable alternatives.** Ironclad Distribution quoted $9,180,000/year (below current rates). Summit Freight quoted $9,540,000/year (+3.7%). Switching cost: $400,000 to $600,000 one-time. Mention this if Redline holds above 6.0%.
