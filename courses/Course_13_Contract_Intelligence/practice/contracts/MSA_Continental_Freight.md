# MSA: Continental Freight

## Parties

This Agreement is entered into between Vanguard Manufacturing Inc. ("Buyer") and Continental Freight ("Supplier").

## Recitals

Less-than-truckload and parcel consolidation services for the aftermarket distribution centers.

## Term

Effective date: 2025-06-20. Expiry date: 2026-06-20. Term: 12 months. Auto-renewal: yes. Notice period: 30 days.

## Compensation

Annual contract value: $450,000. Total contract value over the 12-month term: $450,000.

## Payment

Payment terms: Net 30 from receipt of an undisputed invoice. Invoices may be submitted monthly in arrears.

## Service Levels

Supplier shall meet the service levels set out in Schedule A. Failure to meet a target for two consecutive measurement periods entitles Buyer to service credits as defined in Schedule A.

## Termination

Either party may terminate for convenience on 30 days' written notice. Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not cured within 30 days of written notice.

## Confidentiality

Each party shall hold the other party's Confidential Information in confidence for a period of five years following expiry or termination.

## Intellectual Property Rights

All deliverables and work product created specifically for Buyer under this Agreement are the property of Buyer. Supplier retains its pre-existing intellectual property.

## Insurance Requirements

Supplier shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and shall name Buyer as an additional insured.

## Liability Cap

Supplier's aggregate liability under this Agreement is capped at $750,000, except for breaches of confidentiality, indemnification obligations, and gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## Indemnification

Supplier shall indemnify and hold Buyer harmless from any third-party claims arising out of Supplier's negligence, willful misconduct, or infringement of third-party intellectual property rights.

## Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for delays or failures caused by events beyond reasonable control, including natural disasters, governmental actions, or labor disruptions, provided the affected party gives prompt written notice.

## Audit Rights

Buyer may audit Supplier's records relating to this Agreement on 14 days' written notice, no more than once per calendar year.

## Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

## Obligations

- Maintain motor carrier liability insurance of at least $1,000,000
- Provide real-time shipment tracking through the carrier portal
- Submit a monthly carrier scorecard covering on-time delivery and damage rates
- Notify Vanguard within two hours of any delivery exception
- Complete an annual rate review and benchmark submission
- Maintain valid DOT and FMCSA registrations throughout the term

## Signatures

Executed on 2025-06-20.
