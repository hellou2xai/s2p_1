# Course 3: The Skill Builder

## A typical Monday morning

It is 09:00 Monday. Your CPO has just approved the next sourcing event: a logistics consolidation RFP. You sit down to plan the work.

Six weeks ago you finished a similar RFP for indirect services. You spent two weeks recreating the RFP package from scratch, another week building a custom scorecard, another three days writing the award memo. Total: three and a half weeks of writing methodology you have written four times before.

You open Claude Code. You can do the same thing again. Or you can spend this week building **skills**: reusable methodologies that capture how Acme runs sourcing events. Once. Then every future event configures the inputs and Claude does the work.

That is what Course 3 teaches you to do.

## What a SKILL.md actually is

A SKILL.md is a markdown file that captures a methodology. Not a one-off prompt. A methodology Claude can re-run any number of times, with different inputs, and produce consistent output.

Compared to a prompt:

| Prompt | SKILL.md |
|---|---|
| Written once, used once | Written once, used many times |
| Names specific files | Names input shapes (any RFP, any spend data) |
| Mixed instructions and request | Pure methodology: input format, processing logic, output format, quality criteria |
| Lives in a chat that ends | Lives in `practice/skills/` (or your real `.claude/skills/` folder) |

The five skills you will write in this course are five methodologies your team uses every time it runs a sourcing event:

- `rfp-builder.md` - the RFP package methodology
- `bid-scorer.md` - the weighted scoring methodology
- `award-memo.md` - the recommendation memo structure
- `risk-profiler.md` - the supplier risk assessment framework
- `savings-calculator.md` - the savings methodology

## The practice scenario

Acme Plc is consolidating its logistics carrier base from 14 incumbents down to 4 strategic partners. Annual baseline spend is **11.2m GBP**. Savings target is **1.6m GBP** (about 14% off baseline). The RFP closes 2026-06-15. Award by 2026-06-30.

Today's date is **2026-04-25**. You have:

- A category brief (the sourcing context).
- A supplier longlist of 25 carriers with capability data.
- A spend baseline of around 1,500 historical shipments over the last 12 months.
- Six bid responses from shortlisted bidders (FastRoad UK, Globex Freight, Continental Roads, OceanLine, Pacific Forwarders, Helios Air Freight).
- Skeleton templates for the RFP package, scorecard, and award memo.

By the end of Course 3 you will produce a full sourcing event package by chaining your five new skills against this real-shaped data.

## What you will produce by the end of Course 3

Six files, all generated by skills you wrote yourself:

1. `outputs/category-brief-2026.md` (a polished version of the brief, generated by `rfp-builder` from the input)
2. `outputs/rfp-package.md` (the RFP package generated by `rfp-builder`)
3. `outputs/bid-comparison.md` (the bid scorecard with weighted totals, generated by `bid-scorer`)
4. `outputs/risk-profile.md` (per-bidder risk assessment, generated by `risk-profiler`)
5. `outputs/savings-case.md` (the savings calculation against baseline, generated by `savings-calculator`)
6. `outputs/award-memo.md` (the recommendation memo, generated by `award-memo`, drawing on all the above)

Five skills written once. Six deliverables produced from the same skill library. Every future sourcing event reuses the same skills with new inputs.

## The six lessons

| # | Title | Time |

Total: about 4 hours. Comfortable in two afternoon sessions.

## You are done with the course when

1. Your `practice/skills/` folder has five SKILL.md files. Each one is between 40 and 80 lines.
2. You have run the full chain end to end: started Claude in `practice/`, asked Claude to invoke the five skills in sequence, and produced six deliverables in `practice/outputs/`.
3. The bid comparison names the right top-three bidders (Continental Roads, FastRoad UK, Globex Freight on a price-weighted basis, with Helios Air Freight winning on the air lane and Pacific Forwarders winning on UK-Asia sea).
4. The award memo recommends a four-carrier panel (one road, one road LTL, one sea, one air) with named annual values that sum within 5% of the targeted 1.6m GBP saving.
5. You can copy your skills folder into your real procurement folder at work, swap in your real bid data, and run the next event in 2 hours instead of 3 weeks.
