## Quality criteria

- Portfolio average matches the mean of all 50 supplier overall_scores.
- Category averages match the mean of each category's 10 suppliers.
- Top 10 list is sorted by overall_score descending.
- Every flagged supplier has at least one specific flag reason.
- Recommended actions name specific dates or deadlines, not vague timelines.
- Trend counts sum to 50.
