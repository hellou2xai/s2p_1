# Course 19: Supply Chain Risk and Resilience

## The board wants answers

It is Wednesday morning. Your CPO calls from the airport: "The board meeting moved up to next Thursday. They want the supply chain risk posture and resilience plan. Full briefing. Three pages, not thirty."

You have the data. Spend by item, supplier financials, single-source exposure, approved alternates, and a file of disruption events from the past year. What you do not have is a structured way to turn that data into a board-ready brief in under a week.

Last time, the risk assessment took three analysts two weeks. They built spreadsheets, cross-referenced supplier files, modeled scenarios by hand, and wrote the narrative in a shared doc. The result was 28 pages. The board read the first two.

This course fixes that. You build a risk assessment pipeline that reads your data, scores concentration risk, maps single-source exposure, models disruption scenarios with financial impact, prioritizes mitigations, and produces a board brief. One system. Repeatable every quarter.

## The practice scenario

Fortis Manufacturing is a US-based manufacturer with a 25-supplier portfolio across five categories. The board has asked the CPO to present the supply chain risk posture and resilience plan.

Today's date is **2026-04-25**. You have:

- 25 suppliers with financial health scores (3 have weak financials below 50).
- 200 line items with spend, criticality, and single-source flags.
- 5 single-source items with $2.58M combined annual spend.
- 4 qualified or in-qualification alternates for single-source items.
- 4 historical disruption events with documented impact and recovery.

## The six lessons

| # | Title | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Risk signal architecture: data sources and scoring matrix in CLAUDE.md | 40 min |
| 2 | Concentration risk: supplier, geographic, and specification concentration | 50 min |
| 3 | Single-source exposure mapping: item to supplier to spend to alternate | 45 min |
| 4 | Disruption scenario modeling: named scenario with financial impact | 50 min |
| 5 | Mitigation planning: risk-ranked actions with timeline and cost | 40 min |
| 6 | The board resilience brief: three key findings, top five risks, investment case | 35 min |

Total: about 4.5 hours.

## You are done with the course when

1. You have a risk register with scored risks for all 25 suppliers.
2. You have a single-source exposure map showing 5 items, their suppliers, spend, and alternate status.
3. You have two disruption scenario models with quantified financial impact and recovery timelines.
4. Your board brief has three key findings, top five risks with mitigation status, and a one-paragraph investment case.
5. The full pipeline runs from data to board brief without manual spreadsheet work.
