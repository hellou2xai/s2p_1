# Board Resilience Brief Solution

## Supply Chain Risk Posture and Resilience Plan
**Prepared for**: Board of Directors, Fortis Manufacturing
**Date**: 2026-04-25
**Prepared by**: CPO

### Three key findings

1. **Single-source exposure totals $2,580,000 across 5 items.** Four of five items have a qualified alternate or one in qualification. The exception is ITEM-0025 Microcontroller ARM ($480,000/year) from Apex Electronics (SUP004), which has no qualified alternate and a 10-week qualification time. Apex Electronics' financial health score is 44.8 and the supplier has been in financial distress since November 2025.

2. **Two suppliers carry financial health scores below 50.** Continental Freight (SUP006) at 41.7 and Apex Electronics (SUP004) at 44.8. Together they represent $6,000,000 in annual spend. A failure at either would disrupt production or freight movement within 2 to 4 weeks.

3. **Four disruption events hit the portfolio in the trailing 24 months.** Heartland Polymers (hurricane, 12 days, $420,000 in delayed shipments), Apex Electronics (financial distress, ongoing, lead times doubled), Eagle Transport (logistics capacity crunch, 21 days, $180,000 incremental cost), and CloudBridge Systems (cyber incident, 5 days, $95,000 business interruption). Combined direct cost: $695,000.

### Investment case

We recommend a $340,000 investment in supply chain resilience over the next 12 months:

- **$120,000**: Complete qualification of an alternate source for ITEM-0025 Microcontroller ARM. Timeline: 6 months. Closes the only single-source item with no qualified alternate.
- **$85,000**: Increase safety stock for ITEM-0001 Steel plate 4mm and ITEM-0013 Aluminum sheet 2mm from 14 to 30 days. One-time inventory investment.
- **$45,000**: Run RFP and onboard a backup freight carrier as a hedge against Continental Freight financial risk. Timeline: 3 months.
- **$90,000**: Financial monitoring subscription (quarterly D&B reports for the top 15 suppliers by risk score), with monthly reads on Apex Electronics and Continental Freight.

**Expected risk reduction**: Single-source exposure with no alternate drops from $480,000 to $0. Financially vulnerable spend (suppliers below 50) drops from $6,000,000 to $3,600,000 once the backup carrier is in place.

**Return**: A single avoided disruption at Apex Electronics would prevent an estimated $1,200,000 in production losses and expediting costs (based on the 4 prior events at an average of $174,000 each, scaled for criticality). The $340,000 investment pays for itself in the first prevented event.
