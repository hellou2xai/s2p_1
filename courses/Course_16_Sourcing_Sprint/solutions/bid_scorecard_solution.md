# Bid Scorecard Solution

## Sample: Great Lakes Steel

### Supplier: Great Lakes Steel
**Sub-categories bid**: Steel
**Annual bid value**: $3,780,000 (vs. baseline $4,200,000)

### Scoring

| Criterion | Weight | Raw score (0-100) | Weighted score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price competitiveness | 35% | 78 | 27.3 | $652/ton vs. baseline $724/ton. 9.9% reduction. Volume discount at 6,000+ tons. |
| Quality and reliability | 25% | 85 | 21.3 | ISO 9001 and IATF 16949 certified. Defect rate: 0.3% (below 1% threshold). |
| Delivery performance | 20% | 82 | 16.4 | 14-day lead time. 96.2% on-time delivery (trailing 12 months). |
| Innovation and value-add | 10% | 70 | 7.0 | Offers technical support. No sustainability program documented. |
| Financial stability | 10% | 88 | 8.8 | Revenue $48M. D&B score 82. Insurance meets requirements. |

**Total weighted score**: 80.8 / 100

### Strengths
- Strong price position: 9.9% below baseline, best among incumbents.
- Highest financial stability score among all bidders.
- Consistent quality with 0.3% defect rate.

### Concerns
- Single manufacturing facility (Gary, IN). No backup capacity if disrupted.
- Innovation score below average. No documented sustainability or continuous improvement program.

### Recommendation
Award steel sub-category to Great Lakes Steel. Negotiate a backup capacity clause requiring a documented contingency plan within 90 days of contract execution.
