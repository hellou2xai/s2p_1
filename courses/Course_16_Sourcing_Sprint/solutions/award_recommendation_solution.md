# Award Recommendation Solution

## Award Recommendation: Direct Materials Supply Agreement
**Prepared for**: Lisa Torres, VP of Procurement
**Prepared by**: Senior Category Manager
**Date**: 2026-06-20
**Decision deadline**: 2026-06-25 (board meeting)

### Recommendation

Award the direct materials contract to **Great Lakes Steel** (steel), **Heartland Polymers** (polymers), **Pacific Aluminum** (aluminum), and **Cascade Fasteners** (fasteners and coatings). Dual-source electronic components between **Apex Electronics** (60%) and **Northland Alloys** (40%).

**Total contract value**: $20.1M per year (down from $22.2M baseline).
**Savings**: $2.1M annually (9.5% reduction against baseline spend).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Northland Alloys | 85 | 76 | 74 | 78 | 72 | 78.0 |

### Three recommendations

1. **Award as described above.** Total savings of $2.1M (9.5%) against the $22.2M baseline, exceeding the lower end of the 8-12% target. Dual-sourcing electronics reduces single-source risk on Apex Electronics.

2. **Negotiate performance rebates** with Great Lakes Steel and Heartland Polymers tied to on-time delivery above 97%. Estimated incremental value: $85,000 per year.

3. **Schedule 90-day performance review** for Northland Alloys (new supplier). Set minimum quality threshold at 80/100. If not met, shift volume back to Apex Electronics.
