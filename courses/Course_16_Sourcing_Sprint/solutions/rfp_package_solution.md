# RFP Package Solution

## Request for Proposal: Direct Materials Supply Agreement

### Issuer
Ironbridge Manufacturing, Inc.
Attn: Senior Category Manager
Date issued: 2026-05-02

### Submission deadline
2026-06-06, 5:00 PM CT

### 1. Background

Ironbridge Manufacturing is a US-based industrial company with $22.2M in annual direct materials spend. We are conducting a competitive sourcing event for six sub-categories: steel, polymers, aluminum, electronic components, fasteners, and coatings.

### 2. Scope

| Sub-category | Estimated annual volume | Current annual spend |
|---|---|---|
| Steel | 5,800 tons | $4,200,000 |
| Polymers | 2,050 metric tons | $3,800,000 |
| Aluminum | 1,100,000 sq ft | $3,400,000 |
| Electronic components | 24,200 units | $2,900,000 |
| Fasteners | 3,800,000 units | $2,100,000 |
| Coatings | 180,000 liters | $1,600,000 |

Suppliers may bid on one or more sub-categories. Partial bids are accepted.

### 3. Evaluation criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Price competitiveness | 35% | Unit pricing, total cost of ownership, volume discounts |
| Quality and reliability | 25% | Defect rate, certifications, quality management system |
| Delivery performance | 20% | Lead time, on-time delivery rate, logistics capability |
| Innovation and value-add | 10% | Technical support, continuous improvement, sustainability |
| Financial stability | 10% | Revenue, profitability, credit rating, insurance coverage |

### 4. Submission requirements

Suppliers must provide:
1. Completed pricing schedule (see response template).
2. Quality certifications (ISO 9001 or equivalent).
3. Three customer references (US-based manufacturers, similar volume).
4. Financial statements (most recent audited year).
5. Delivery capability statement including lead times by sub-category.

### 6. Terms and conditions

- Contract term: 24 months with option to extend 12 months.
- Payment terms: Net 45 days.
- Annual price adjustment: CPI plus 1%, capped at 5%.
- Performance SLAs: on-time delivery above 95%, defect rate below 1%.
- Insurance: Commercial general liability $5,000,000, product liability $2,000,000.
