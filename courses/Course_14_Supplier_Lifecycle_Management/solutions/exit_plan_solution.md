# Exit Plan Solution

## Sample: Regional Supply Co (SUP023)

**Category**: Raw materials
**Tier**: Standard
**Current stage**: Exit

Regional Supply Co has failed to meet quality and delivery standards for three consecutive quarters. Two corrective action plans were completed without sustained improvement. Overall score declined from 3.4 to 2.2 over six months.

### Risk summary
- **Supply continuity**: Low risk. Cascade Fasteners has capacity and already supplies similar items.
- **Price impact**: Medium risk. Cascade's fastener pricing is 6% higher than Regional Supply Co. Annual impact: approximately $18,000 on a $300,000 annual spend line.
- **Quality**: Positive. Cascade's quality score is 4.3 versus Regional Supply Co's 2.2.
