# Regulatory Update: Section 301 Tariff Adjustments

**Publisher:** US Trade Policy Bulletin
**Published:** 2026-04-08
**Effective:** 2026-07-01

## Summary

A 15 percent additional tariff applies to imported industrial gasket materials and select polymer compounds (HTS 4016.93 and 3920.99) starting 2026-07-01. The tariff covers EPDM, FKM, and select fluoropolymer formulations sourced from Asia.

## Scope

- Affected categories: industrial gaskets, sealing rings, polymer sheet stock for fabricated parts.
- Country coverage: China, Vietnam, Malaysia.
- Estimated landed cost increase for affected SKUs: 11 to 17 percent.

## Recommended action

Review supplier base for affected SKUs. Identify domestic or Mexico-based alternatives for any line where the imported share exceeds 40 percent of annual volume. Pre-buy a 90-day inventory buffer before the 2026-07-01 effective date if a domestic switch cannot be completed in time.
