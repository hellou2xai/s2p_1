# Supplier Capability Benchmark, Spring 2026 Trade Show

**Publisher:** Atlas Manufacturing Sourcing team (internal)
**Source event:** Industrial Sealing and Hose Expo, Cleveland, 2026-04-09 to 2026-04-11
**Published:** 2026-04-16

## Summary

Three suppliers demonstrated new capabilities relevant to Atlas Manufacturing's industrial gasket and hydraulic fitting categories.

## Supplier observations

1. **Crescent Sealing Co. (Akron, Ohio).** Launched a domestic FKM compounding line with 1,200 tons annual capacity. Lead time 14 to 21 days. ISO 9001 and AS9100 certified. Currently not on Atlas approved supplier list.

2. **Northland Hydraulics (Minneapolis, Minnesota).** Announced vendor-managed inventory program for hydraulic fittings, minimum order $25,000 per quarter. Eight Atlas plants are within their service radius.

3. **PolyForm Industries (Houston, Texas).** Expanded EPDM gasket production by 40 percent. Now offers six-week lead time on custom dimensions versus 10 to 12 weeks industry standard.

## Recommended action

Add Crescent Sealing Co. and PolyForm Industries to the approved supplier list before the Q3 RFP cycle. Pilot the Northland Hydraulics VMI program at one plant for 90 days before broader rollout.
