# Steel Price Outlook, Q2 2026

**Publisher:** SteelMarketWatch (commodity research service)
**Published:** 2026-04-12
**Coverage:** US flat-rolled steel

## Headline

Hot-rolled coil prices are forecast to rise 8 to 12 percent through Q3 2026, driven by infrastructure spending and constrained Midwest mill capacity. Cold-rolled is expected to follow at a 4 to 6 percent increase.

## Price drivers

- Federal infrastructure outlays releasing $14.2B in awarded projects between April and August 2026.
- US Steel and Nucor have planned maintenance shutdowns in June, removing roughly 320,000 tons of monthly capacity.
- Chinese export quotas reduced by 8 percent for H2 2026, tightening global supply.

## Forecast

- Q2 2026 hot-rolled: $720 to $760 per ton (currently $702).
- Q3 2026 hot-rolled: $740 to $790 per ton.
- Q4 2026 hot-rolled: flat to slightly down if housing starts soften.

## Recommended action

Forward-buy 60 to 90 days of hot-rolled coverage at current contract pricing before the Q2 mill maintenance window. Reassess in late June.
